Our promise

The handover

Four pillars of a clean exit

1

Capability transfer

Train-the-trainer and "Learn → Do → Teach" enablement so your people — not ours — run the factory. We cement behavior change in the Teach phase.

2

Runbooks & documentation

Operating rhythms, architecture decisions, prompts, evals and skills live where your teams and agents can learn from them — not in tribal knowledge.

3

IP & data boundary

Data residency, tenant isolation and model independence mean you can swap any single model without losing what makes you distinct. Your IP stays yours.

4

Governance & security controls

Agent identity, risk-based permissions and a governed lifecycle so every action stays traceable, auditable and reversible after we're gone.
