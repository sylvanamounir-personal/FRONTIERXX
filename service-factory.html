The idea

A factory, not a pile of pilots

The visible change may be an agent, but the invisible work underneath is what matters: the shared source of truth, the reusable orchestration layer, the telemetry, the governance, and the private evals. Together they form a learning system where your evals define "good," your context steers the work, and every cycle of feedback improves the system against your standards.

If you don't define what "good" looks like, AI defaults to something generic. Private evals are how AI becomes a compounding advantage.— Codify your advantage, Frontier Firm Playbook

The build

Seven steps to a working AI factory

We stand these up incrementally, proving value at each stage.

1

Shared context & MCP layer

A governed interface so agents securely read and write live enterprise context — no brittle custom integrations.

2

Private evals

Translate your "secret sauce" — point of view, performance data, taste, guardrails — into concrete, testable standards.

3

Context & harness

The runtimes, tools and workflows that steer execution, deliberately decoupled from any single foundation model.

4

Agent & skill library

Reusable agents, skills and prompts that publish to a shared library so each sandbox feeds the next.

5

Reinforcement loop

Feedback, scoring and tuning so the system hill-climbs toward your standards every cycle.

6

Telemetry & measurement

An input → throughput → output → outcome pipeline that proves adoption is turning into business value.

7

Security & governance by design

Agent identity, risk-based permissions, data residency, tenant isolation and model independence — so your intelligence compounds inside your boundary, not someone else's.

How we measure it

From adoption to impact

You cannot measure what you cannot see. We instrument the pipeline end to end and map the dependencies between layers.

LayerWhat it measuresImpact timeline
InputAdoption, workflow efficiency, readiness — who is using AI, where, how often2–4 weeks
ThroughputHow AI is applied and integrated — signals of real workflow penetration1–3 months
OutputProductivity & performance improvements resulting from AI use3–6 months
OutcomeHigh-level business impact against your frontier goals6+ months
Diagnostic by design. If input metrics are healthy but throughput isn't moving in three to six months, you have a habit-formation problem — not a tooling problem. The measurement model tells you which.
